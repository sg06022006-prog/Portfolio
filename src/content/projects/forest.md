  - My goal was to practice scene composition. I focused on camera placement, lighting, and arranging objects within the frame, learning how these choices work together in the final image.
  - This project was an opportunity to focus on building a scene rather than modeling every asset from scratch. The part I like most about the result is its atmosphere.
