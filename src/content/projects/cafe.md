  - Cafe is one of my first practice scenes in Blender. I followed a tutorial because I had no basic skills at the time. My goal was to become familiar with the software and work through the main steps of creating a scene.
  - During the project, I practiced moving and editing objects, building geometry, and applying textures. I tried to complete each step on my own and returned to the tutorial whenever I needed more explanation.
  - Modifiers and UV unwrapping were the most challenging parts. I was still learning how they worked, especially how to prepare a model's surface for textures. This scene gave me my first practical experience with both.
  - I made the scene to learn, without aiming for a particular mood. What I liked most about the result was the building itself and the lighting. The project gave me my first experience of putting together a complete 3D scene.
